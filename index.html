Try the main scenario

BiteGuard watches how fast you eat and gives your wrist a gentle buzz before you overeat — no calorie counting needed. Follow the six steps on the phone; nothing here needs an account.

Where the data goes

Each action below shows the row it would write to the BiteGuard PostgreSQL database from Assignment 3 (tables meals, sensor_readings, alerts, meal_items, device_settings, device_events). In this prototype the writes are shown, not sent: the bracelet and its sensor stream are simulated in your browser.
